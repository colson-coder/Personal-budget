Babylon's Purse ("the app") is run by [OWNER NAME]. This page explains what
data the app handles and why.

## The short version
- Your financial data is yours. We don't sell it, rent it or use it for ads.
- Without an account, everything stays in your browser on your device, and we
  never see it.
- With an account, your data is stored so that only you can read it.

## What we store
**Device-only mode (no account):** everything you enter (transactions,
categories, budgets, funds, debts, settings) is saved in your browser's local
storage. It never reaches our servers. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

**With an account:** your email address, and the data you enter, stored with
our database provider, Supabase. Database rules let each account read and change
only its own rows.

**Exchange rates:** to convert currencies, the app asks a rates service for the
rate on a date. Only the currency and date are sent, never your amounts.

**Purchase auto-logging (optional):** if you set up the phone shortcut, your
phone sends the amount and shop name of each tap-to-pay purchase to the app,
which saves it to your data like any entry you type. No bank login is involved.

**AI coach (if offered):** when you ask the coach a question, the question and a
summary of your totals (not individual purchases) are sent to our AI provider
to write the answer. [Confirm: the provider's data retention terms.]

## What we don't do
- No ads, no selling data, no tracking across other websites.
- We don't ask for bank passwords.

## Your choices
- **Export:** Settings → Your data → download a full backup or CSV, anytime.
- **Delete:** delete individual entries in the app, or email us to delete your
  account and all its data. We do this within 30 days.
